About PAM Event

Every detail, considered. Every moment, felt.

Founded by planner & designer Pamela "Pam" Jean-Louis, PAM Event grew from a one-woman styling studio into a full-service team of planners, designers, and day-of coordinators serving clients across Haiti and the diaspora.

We believe an event should feel effortless for the people living it — even when it took a hundred details to get there. From the first mood board to the final farewell, our team handles logistics, vendors, design, and timeline so you can stay present in the moment.

  • Dedicated lead planner from booking to breakdown
  • In-house decor, floral, and tent/rental inventory
  • Trusted network of caterers, photographers & musicians
  • Bilingual team — Kreyòl, Français, English
