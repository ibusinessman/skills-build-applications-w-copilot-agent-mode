# Meta Ads — promotion rules

A winner from `analytics/winners.md` gets promoted ONLY if it passes ALL of these:

## Thresholds (must meet all)
- Organic reach ≥ 500 on at least one platform
- Engagement rate ≥ 6%
- Save rate ≥ 2% (signal of real value, not just entertainment)
- Post is ≤ 7 days old

## Content rules (must pass all)
- Does NOT make unverifiable superlative claims ("the best", "#1")
- Does NOT use before/after imagery (Meta policy)
- Has a clear CTA that matches a live URL in `company/offers.md`
- Voice matches `company/brand.md`

## Budget
- Starting daily budget per campaign: $20
- Max concurrent promoted posts: 3
- Pause a campaign if CPC > $5 after 48 hours spend

## Ad account
See `meta-ads/ad-account.md` for the Meta Ad Account ID and credentials.
